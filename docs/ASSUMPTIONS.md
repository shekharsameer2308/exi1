# Model Assumptions & Physical Boundaries

**Framework**: E-Methanol Packed-Bed Membrane Reactor  
**Classification**: Level 2 / Level 3 (1D Transport Model)  

---

## 1. Fluid Dynamics & Packed Bed Assumptions
1. **1D Plug Flow**: Neglects radial velocity and concentration gradients across the bed diameter ($D = 0.02\text{ m}$).
2. **Negligible Axial Dispersion**: High Peclet numbers ($Pe > 100$) justify the differential plug flow assumption.
3. **Pseudo-Homogeneous Phase**: Solid catalyst particles and interstitial gas phase are treated as a pseudo-continuous reacting mixture.
4. **Ideal Gas Mixture**: Valid at operating temperatures ($200 - 260^\circ\text{C}$) and moderate pressures ($30 - 80\text{ bar}$).

---

## 2. Reaction Kinetics & Catalysis
1. **Primary Reactions**: Methanol synthesis occurs predominantly from direct $\text{CO}_2$ hydrogenation, with $\text{CO}$ produced via the endothermic RWGS reaction.
2. **LHHW Surface Mechanism**: Adsorption equilibrium is assumed for surface intermediates on $\text{Cu/ZnO/Al}_2\text{O}_3$.
3. **No External Film Diffusion Resistance**: Interphase mass transfer between bulk gas and catalyst surface is fast relative to intrinsic kinetics.

---

## 3. Membrane Permeation & Transport
1. **Hydrophilic Zeolite (NaA)**: High selectivity toward water vapor ($\alpha_{\text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{CO}_2} \ge 1000$, $\alpha_{\text{H}_2\text{O}/\text{H}_2} \ge 500$).
2. **Radial Outward Flux Only**: Permeation occurs from high-pressure reaction tube into the low-pressure shell/sweep stream.
3. **Sweep Gas Sink**: Counter-current sweep flow maintains low water partial pressure on the permeate side.

---

## 4. Heat Transfer & Energy Balance
1. **Wall Heat Exchange**: Heat generated by exothermic methanol synthesis is removed through the reactor wall via coolant fluid at $T_{\text{cool}}$.
2. **Uniform Cross-Sectional Temperature**: Radial temperature gradients are lumped into the overall heat transfer coefficient $U$.
